Welcome to Mathura

Mathura is a beautiful and historic city in Uttar Pradesh. It is famous for its rich cultural heritage, temples, traditions and connection with Lord Krishna.

Famous Places

Krishna Janmabhoomi

A famous religious and historical site located in the heart of Mathura.

Vishram Ghat

A beautiful ghat situated on the banks of the Yamuna River.

Dwarkadhish Temple

One of the well-known temples of Mathura, famous for its beautiful architecture.

Culture & Tradition

Mathura has a vibrant culture filled with music, festivals, traditional food and religious celebrations.

Famous Festival:

Holi in Mathura and the nearby Braj region is celebrated with great enthusiasm and attracts visitors from different parts of the country.
